Group C 2026 World Cup Schedule: Full Dates, Kickoff Times, and Printable PDF

  • Post author:Jacey Tine
  • Post published:May 1, 2026
  • Post category:Soccer

Group C at the 2026 FIFA World Cup is the East Coast group, with all six matches scheduled across U.S. venues from New Jersey to Florida. Brazil arrives as the five-time champion and headliner, joined by 2022 semifinalist Morocco, a Haiti side returning to the World Cup for the first time since 1974, and a Scotland team back on the global stage after a 28-year absence. The group opens on Saturday, June 13, with Brazil and Morocco at MetLife Stadium, then closes with simultaneous matches on Wednesday, June 24, when Scotland faces Brazil in Miami Gardens and Morocco meets Haiti in Atlanta.

Group C Match Schedule and Standings

Every Group C match is in the United States, with the schedule rotating through MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, and Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. Brazil is the favorite to top the group on paper, but Morocco’s run to the 2022 semifinals and Scotland’s defensive style give the group plenty of upset potential. The top two finishers automatically advance to the knockout rounds, while the third-place team will hope to finish among the eight best thirds across all 12 groups to grab one of the remaining Round of 32 spots.

The Path to the Final: Group C Knockout Scenarios

The group stage does not end Group C’s run on June 24. The top two finishers, plus a possible third-place advancer, move into the single-elimination Round of 32. Here is the path a Group C team must take to reach the final in New York/New Jersey:

  • Round of 32: July 2 – July 7, 2026
  • Round of 16: July 9 – July 12, 2026
  • Quarterfinals: July 14 – July 15, 2026
  • Semifinals: July 17 – July 18, 2026
  • World Cup Final: July 19, 2026 (MetLife Stadium)
